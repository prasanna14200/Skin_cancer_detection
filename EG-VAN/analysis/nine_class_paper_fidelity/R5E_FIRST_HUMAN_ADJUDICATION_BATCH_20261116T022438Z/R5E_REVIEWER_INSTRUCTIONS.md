# R5E Reviewer Instructions

## Decision options
- `SAME_PHOTO`: same underlying photograph, including resized, compressed, cropped, or color-adjusted versions where identity is clear.
- `DISTINCT_PHOTOS`: different photographs, even if they depict the same lesion or subject.
- `UNCERTAIN`: evidence is insufficient to decide confidently.

## Evidence reviewers may use
- Original queue fields, file paths, split labels, and AI assessment notes.
- The images themselves in the visual review pages created for this package.
- Existing project evidence ledgers and image metadata already recorded in the review package.

## What reviewers must not do
- Do not use AI scores as ground truth.
- Do not prefill decisions using filenames, automated similarity, or queue order.
- Do not merge, delete, relabel, or modify the original records.
- Do not infer patient identity or lesion identity from visual appearance.
