# R4 Research Completion Roadmap

## Phase 1: data and provenance closure
- Obtain a versioned, rights-cleared source bundle with record-level lineage.
- Resolve patient/lesion identity, duplicates and leakage exposure.
- Freeze an approved nine-class split with documented hash and class counts.

## Phase 2: methodology and fidelity closure
- Resolve augmentation formulation, symbol definitions and count ledger.
- Confirm the exact paper-implemented branch and training configuration subject to evidence.
- Decide which parts of the project are exploratory versus paper-faithful.

## Phase 3: approved training and validation
- Train on the approved split under the frozen pipeline.
- Record best and last checkpoints, curves and confusion matrices.
- Produce metrics on a genuine held-out set with source evidence.

## Phase 4: research-extension validation
- Run the IQA analysis on the approved hold-out.
- Run uncertainty and calibration workflows on the approved split only.
- Pursue external validation only if a valid eligible cohort exists.

## Completion condition
The research is not complete until all data provenance, split integrity, model performance and method-specific analyses are supported by approved artifacts and clear labeling. Until then, the project remains in an evidence-blocked state.
