# Task 6C Rubric Ambiguities

## Status

No reviewer completed the fictional scenarios, so no ambiguities were observed during a calibration discussion. The following potential wording issues are carried forward from the read-only Task 6B protocol review; they remain proposed clarifications, not adopted changes.

1. **`UNCERTAIN` versus `NOT_COMPARABLE`:** clarify that `NOT_COMPARABLE` requires a concrete access, integrity, or usability problem, while usable observations with unresolved relationship evidence remain `UNCERTAIN`.
2. **Confidence on unresolved decisions:** clarify that confidence for `UNCERTAIN` describes confidence that evidence is insufficient, not certainty about the underlying relationship. For `NOT_COMPARABLE`, it describes confidence in the inability to compare.
3. **Two-reviewer exceptions:** harmonize the universal two-reviewer requirement with the “where feasible” wording for `NOT_COMPARABLE`; any exception should be documented and labeled preliminary.
4. **Adjudication blinding:** explicitly keep AI, automated similarity, and source metadata hidden until the adjudicator's visual outcome is locked.
5. **`DISTINCT` scope:** emphasize that the decision applies only to the reviewed pair and does not establish patient independence or clear other possible links.
6. **Confidence and escalation:** make the handling of LOW-confidence categorical answers consistent; weak evidence must not become a forced category through adjudication.

These are documentation-based observations only. No fictional response patterns or reviewer behavior were available to evaluate.
