Evidence retrieval date: 2026-11-22. This is a source inventory, not legal advice or approval. Retrieval failures and successful alternatives are noted rather than treated as evidence.

## HAM10000

- **Original paper:** [PMC6091241](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6091241/), Tschandl, Rosendahl & Kittler, *The HAM10000 dataset*, Scientific Data 5, 180161 (2018), DOI [10.1038/sdata.2018.161](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.161). Table 1 lists HAM10000 as **CC BY-NC 4.0**, 10,015 images. The article notice separately licenses the article under CC BY 4.0 and says CC0 applies to “the metadata files made available in this article.” This article-metadata statement does not identify the dataset's `HAM10000_metadata.csv`.
- **Versioned official repository API:** [Harvard Dataverse v4.0 metadata](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/datasets/:persistentId/versions/4.0/metadata?persistentId=doi%3A10.7910%2FDVN%2FDBW86T). Retrieved via `curl.exe`; JSON `status=OK`, `schema:version=4.0`, `schema:datePublished=2018-06-04`. Exact `data.dvcore:termsOfUse` heading: `Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International Public License`; that field contains the full license text. The response has no top-level `license` property. `data.dvcore:fileTermsOfAccess.dvcore:fileRequestAccess` is `false` (access request setting, not license). See `HAM10000_DATAVERSE_V4_API_EVIDENCE.json`.
- **Versioned landing page:** [Harvard Dataverse dataset record](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/DBW86T). Direct browser/web-fetch retrieval failed in this environment; the versioned API endpoint above succeeded.
- **Article/data scope:** the article's CC BY 4.0 notice concerns the article and excludes third-party materials identified by credit line. Its CC0 waiver concerns metadata files made available in that article. Neither statement is used to infer a CC BY or CC0 license for the dataset images or local metadata CSV.
- **License text:** [CC BY-NC 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en). NonCommercial is defined as use not primarily intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation; sharing/adaptation are for NonCommercial purposes only. Attribution and modification indications are required when sharing. The license grants only rights the licensor can grant; other rights may still apply.
- **Local matching:** `analysis/nine_class_exploratory/exploratory_split.csv` contains 9,310 unique HAM IDs, all present in `data/raw/HAM10000_metadata.csv`. This is not byte identity to the Dataverse release or per-image rights verification.

## SIIM-ISIC 2020

- **Aggregate Challenge terms and citation:** [official 2020 data page](https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/#2020), opened through the official [dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.34970/2020-ds01). The page lists 33,126 training records, metadata v2 (including patient and lesion IDs), and links aggregate `CC-BY-NC` to [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). It states the aggregate data must be cited as: International Skin Imaging Collaboration. SIIM-ISIC 2020 Challenge Dataset. International Skin Imaging Collaboration. DOI 10.34970/2020-ds01 (2020). Direct web-fetch to the Challenge host was blocked; browser navigation through the DOI loaded the official page.
- **ISIC Terms of Use:** [official Terms of Use](https://challenge.isic-archive.com/terms-of-use/), opened by browser navigation from the official Challenge page. The terms say site access/use signifies assent and the site may be used without creating an account; image upload or annotation requires an account. Contributors select CC0, BY, or BY-NC for each submitted image, and images may be used only in conjunction with that contributor license. The terms separately say annotations are CC0 and the ISIC Site Database is licensed under CC0; these statements are not treated as blanket image licenses or as an interpretation of the Challenge aggregate license.
- **Per-image API evidence:** each of the 79 local IDs was fetched from its exact `https://api.isic-archive.com/api/v2/images/<ISIC_ID>/` record. Live `isic_id`, `copyright_license`, and `attribution` matched the local acquisition register for **79/79**. Values split into 41 `CC-BY` and 38 `CC-BY-NC`. API license codes do not state a version number. Details are in `SIIM_API_RECORD_REVALIDATION.csv`.
- **Challenge package identity:** the official data page links the fixed [2020 Training JPEG ZIP](https://isic-archive.s3.amazonaws.com/challenges/2020/ISIC_2020_Training_JPEG.zip). The local project used Archive API/S3 image records; it did not acquire or byte-compare the fixed Challenge ZIP.
- **License texts:** [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en) and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) were directly retrieved. The former defines NonCommercial, grants sharing/adaptation only for NonCommercial purposes, and requires attribution/modification indications when sharing. The licenses grant only rights the licensor can grant and do not resolve other rights or whether model artifacts are derivatives.
