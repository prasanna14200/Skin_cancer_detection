# Stage 6 dataset-resolution decision

Date: 2026-10-22. The nine source-defined categories remain the required research objective. This decision reads the frozen Stage 3I review, Stage 4 source inventory and previous provenance audits; it makes **no** image eligibility decision, exclusion, split or clinical relabeling. The [read-only readiness audit](../analysis/nine_class_stage6/dataset_readiness_audit.json) records input hashes and current counts.

## What is established

The local inventory has 10,015 HAM10000 entries and 3,297 canonical Fanconic-folder entries. The mirrored Fanconic tree is excluded. HAM metadata supplies image and lesion IDs, but no verified patient IDs; the local Fanconic collection supplies only folder labels and publisher `train`/`test` partitions. Its numeric filenames are not ISIC or patient identifiers. The previous [Fanconic provenance audit](NINE_CLASS_DATASET_RECOVERY_PLAN.md) matched current Kaggle v4 file **names and byte sizes**, but did not obtain remote per-file hashes or identify the version used in the EG-VAN experiment. The publisher-supplied test folder does not establish an EG-VAN evaluation protocol.

The [Stage 3I AI-assisted review](NINE_CLASS_STAGE3I_AI_REVIEW.md) assigned 83 `AI_SAME_PHOTO`, 326 `AI_DISTINCT` and 20 `AI_UNCERTAIN` **pair** assessments. All 83 provisionally retained BENIGN **records** have AI-supported HAM photographic candidates; 19 belong to the publisher source-test partition. The prior exclusion ledger proposes 1,717 BENIGN and 652 MALIGNANT exclusions (2,369 total). None has been silently approved. Stage 4 records **zero `APPROVED_ELIGIBLE` entries** and no final split. Its conservative graph includes suspected relations and HAM lesion links; 90 graph groups span publisher train/test after those links are joined. Graph membership is a split precaution, not proof of shared patients.

The 83 strong AI judgments have geometric and visual support but no independently verified Fanconic source image IDs or completed second human adjudication. An AI `DISTINCT` judgment does not establish patient or lesion independence. The single historical unexplained human `SAME_PHOTO` choice is insufficient for a broad exclusion policy. No local source README, original download receipt, versioned image checksum list, Fanconic image-to-ISIC map, patient/lesion key, clinical label derivation, or image-level rights record was found. These gaps cannot be resolved by filenames, hashes or model code.

## Source options and label meaning

| Route | What it could support | Conditions before use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: Current HAM + Fanconic family | Closest available source-family adaptation to the paper. At most 83 BENIGN and 845 MALIGNANT binary entries remain after **proposed** exclusions; these are upper bounds. | Independently adjudicate all retained BENIGN relations and publisher test conflicts, approve a source-conditioned label protocol and per-image eligibility, establish a protected test with meaningful BENIGN support, and resolve source/version/rights limits. If all 83 AI same-photo judgments are confirmed and approved as exclusions, the local BENIGN class becomes empty. |
| B: Add an independently documented binary cohort | Potentially restore a nine-label **adaptation** if it supplies genuinely additional, compatible cases. | Verify original clinical definitions, patient/lesion identifiers, image rights, source partition, and nonoverlap with HAM/Fanconic before download, merging or assignment. Do not map HAM diagnoses into broad labels or assume Fanconic's undefined broad labels equal another source's binary target. |
| External validation source | Could test transportability after a valid internal nine-class cohort exists. | Predefine label mapping and supported outputs, check patient/photo overlap, and report missing or incompatible classes. An external binary-only cohort cannot by itself provide a nine-class internal test. |

The strongest **conditional** official additional-source candidate remains [SIIM–ISIC 2020](https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/). The official challenge publishes an aggregate CC BY-NC 4.0 license and a melanoma-classification task with clinical context; its research paper describes patient-centric metadata. Its melanoma-versus-benign-mimicker scope is narrower than Fanconic's undefined `MALIGNANT` label. It is therefore **not an approved plug-in replacement** and no additional eligible count is known. The [ISIC Archive terms](https://www.isic-archive.com/terms-conditions) state that individual Archive images use their contributors' licenses, so a source-level label or paper license does not settle rights for the local Fanconic JPEGs. The [Fanconic uploader page](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fanconic/skin-cancer-malignant-vs-benign) describes processed ISIC pictures but supplies no verified local image-ID bridge. No dataset was downloaded in Stage 6.

The nine targets combine seven HAM diagnostic labels with two broad labels defined by their separate source. They are **not nine mutually exclusive clinical diagnoses**. A HAM melanoma can also be malignant; a HAM nevus can also be benign. Any eventual model score must be described as source-conditioned classification, with per-source diagnostics and source-cue checks, unless an independently justified clinical ontology is established.

## Decision and required evidence

**Current nine-class trainability: NO. Current leakage-audited nine-class internal evaluation: NO.** The current source inventory has no approved split, independent retained BENIGN count, or defensible protected nine-class test. The 19 source-test BENIGN upper-bound entries all need identity resolution; transferring them or their linked groups into training would violate the protected boundary. Metadata and image similarity alone cannot settle their patient identities.

Before freezing a cohort, obtain: (1) independent image-pair adjudication with preserved reviewer evidence, including the 83 BENIGN candidates and 20 uncertain relations; (2) source/version and per-image label/rights evidence, ideally the EG-VAN authors' package and split record; (3) a written decision on source-conditioned label meaning and whether an additional documented source is compatible; (4) an approved eligibility ledger and group-aware protected split with all nine classes; and (5) a signed-off approval record tied to exact inventory/split hashes. Software can check the resulting files mechanically; it cannot certify that a person's identity decision or license conclusion is scientifically correct. Full training and final test use remain withheld until those gates are met.
