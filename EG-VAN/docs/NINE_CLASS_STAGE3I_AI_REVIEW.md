# Stage 3I — AI-assisted review of 429 image pairs

Date: 2026-10-19. **Nine-class EG-VAN remains the research objective:** HAM10000 `AKIEC, BCC, BKL, DF, MEL, NV, VASC` plus the source-defined Fanconic `BENIGN, MALIGNANT`. This is an **AI-assisted photographic comparison**, not independent human adjudication, clinical ground truth, an exclusion decision, or a final splitting protocol. No source label, original image, earlier audit CSV, or human-review ledger was changed.

## Inputs and visual procedure

The frozen input set is the 429 unique candidate pair IDs in the [existing human ledger](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/benign_pair_review_ledger.csv), matched exactly to [Stage 3F geometry](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/geometric_pair_evidence.csv). The first 83 use the [Stage 3G identity evidence](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3g/strong_pair_identity_evidence.csv). Existing pHash/dHash, original-size pixel MAE/correlation, geometry method, inliers and failure reasons were read; no detector was retrained or threshold calibrated. The single pre-existing human `SAME_PHOTO` choice was **not used** to assign the AI status.

The [sheet generator](../src/nine_class_stage3i_sheets.py) opened **both original JPEGs for every pair**, verified each source path, and recorded SHA-256 and original dimensions in the [429-row sheet manifest](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/sheet_manifest.csv). It rendered 36 numbered [original-image contact sheets](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/sheets/) in the required order: 19 strong publisher source-test pairs, 64 other strong pairs, then 346 remaining relations. It also arranged the existing Stage 3G overlays into seven [overlay review sheets](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/overlay_sheets/). The Codex vision capability inspected **all 36 original-image sheets and all seven overlay sheets**, including lesion fields and hair/ruler/artifact patterns; native original-resolution files for the projective-warp outlier and a hair-dominated pair were additionally inspected. Contact-sheet views are scaled (maximum 282×240 pixels per original image), so this review does **not** claim native-resolution visual inspection of all 858 image appearances or reliable same-lesion determination. Every pair was visually displayed and inspected; **zero** rows are based on numbers alone.

The frozen [AI review CSV](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/ai_pair_review.csv) records one row per pair ID: exact original paths and hashes, visual category, image evidence summary, visual landmark explanation, all relevant similarity/geometry fields, conflicting evidence, uncertainty reason, cross-check status, sheet and Stage 3G visual references, review method, model family and its version limitation, configuration version and UTC timestamp. The model identifier is recorded as `Codex, GPT-6 family (exact vision build/version not exposed)`; no numerical confidence was invented. [The freeze script](../src/nine_class_stage3i_review.py) contains the fixed visual annotation order and the explicit list of 20 visually unresolved alternatives. It validates all source hashes and refuses to overwrite its output. Reproducing the *files* from the same frozen annotation is possible; reproducing an AI vision judgment is limited by model-build availability and contact-sheet presentation.

## Meaningfully different automated cross-check

Stage 3F ORB/RANSAC feature matching, with SIFT fallback and recorded transformation checks, is a geometric method distinct from this AI visual comparison. It supplies **coherent photographic support for all 83 AI same-photo pairs**, though Stage 3G flags one projective plausibility outlier. For the other **346**, geometry was unreliable or insufficient; it cannot independently verify either the 326 AI distinct assessments or the 20 uncertain ones. The 20 unresolved rows explicitly preserve this visual/pixel-versus-geometry tension. The visual reviewer had access to geometry, so this is a **different algorithmic cross-check**, not a blind or independent reviewer. No detector precision, recall, false-positive rate or false-negative rate can be calculated from these AI judgments; independently verified hard negatives and sufficient held-out real positives remain unavailable.

## Conservative overlap graph and Stage 4 implications

The [proposed overlap edges](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/proposed_overlap_edges.csv) preserve three evidence tiers: prior established photograph relations from the existing [proposed exclusion audit](../analysis/nine_class_overlap_completion/proposed_binary_exclusions.csv), **AI-supported** same-photo relations, and **suspected unresolved** relations. The [transitive proposed groups](../analysis/nine_class_recovery/stage3i/proposed_overlap_groups.csv) contain **2,444 groups** across **2,477 unique tiered edges**. **82 groups** contain an AI-supported same-photo edge; **6 groups** contain a suspected unresolved edge; **4 groups** include both publisher source-test and source-train images. Group IDs are deterministic SHA-256 digests of sorted member paths. These groups are **planning evidence only**. Do not automatically exclude their images or infer patient independence from group membership.

If Stage 4 later proceeds with an approved dataset, confirmed photo duplicates must follow the prior deterministic exclusion policy. AI-supported photo and unresolved suspected relations should remain in **one partition** or be **quarantined from primary evaluation** pending adjudication. Where a group touches the Fanconic publisher source-test boundary, protect that boundary; do not transfer its training partner into training while the relation is unresolved. An AI `DISTINCT` assessment does not certify that different records come from different patients or lesions.

The current proposed remaining Fanconic BENIGN upper bound stays **83** (64 publisher train, 19 publisher test), because Stage 3I applied **zero** new exclusions. All **19** provisionally retained publisher-test BENIGN records have an AI-supported same-photo HAM relation. If later independent adjudication approves all 83 AI-supported photo relations for exclusion, **zero** of these BENIGN records would remain; this is a conditional scenario, not the current eligible count. The other **845** provisionally retained MALIGNANT records and their identity limitations are unchanged. The 326 AI distinct pair judgments do not establish 326 eligible cases, because multiple candidate pairs refer to the same BENIGN record.

**A defensible nine-class final test split cannot be frozen now.** The publisher source-test BENIGN upper bound is small and photographically suspect; the Fanconic version used by EG-VAN, image-level clinical provenance, patient/lesion IDs and image rights remain unverified. Keep the nine-class goal. Assess an additional, documented, label-compatible source under the [Stage 3H source decision](NINE_CLASS_STAGE3H_DECISION.md) if independent review eliminates the Fanconic BENIGN class. No images were downloaded or merged here.
